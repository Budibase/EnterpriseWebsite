# Budibase Brand

Apply the canonical Budibase strategy consistently while adapting the execution
to the audience, channel, and task.

## Required context

Before acting, read `../../../docs/brand/budibase-brand-and-gtm.md` completely.
Treat it as authoritative for positioning, audience, differentiators,
personality, visual direction, messaging, proof, guardrails, and reference
brands.

For reviews, audits, final quality checks, or substantial new customer-facing
work, also read `references/review-checklist.md`.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience, operational problem, desired outcome, channel, and
   stage of the buying journey.
2. Identify the evidence available for every important claim. Do not invent
   customer approval, metrics, quotations, certifications, security guarantees,
   or product capabilities.
3. Choose the relevant parts of the canonical strategy. Do not force every
   differentiator into every deliverable.
4. Create or revise the work with operational freedom and IT control held
   together. Make the operational job concrete and make enterprise control
   visible.
5. Preserve the requested format, existing design system, and channel norms
   unless they conflict with the strategy or the user asks for a new direction.
6. Run the review checklist before delivery. Resolve material weaknesses or
   state what evidence or decision is missing.

## Task guidance

### Create or edit copy

- Lead with the operational outcome rather than the product category.
- Name the audience or environment when it improves relevance.
- Use precise nouns, active verbs, and short declarative headlines.
- Explain how Budibase works and why IT can trust it.
- Replace generic no-code language and AI hype with concrete capabilities,
  controls, and outcomes.

### Create or edit design

- Use Budibase's forest-green identity and condensed display typography with
  restraint and intent.
- Prefer product screens, workflows, architecture, integrations, controls, and
  operational evidence over decorative abstraction or generic photography.
- Use color semantically and motion to explain cause and effect.
- Preserve strong grids, clear hierarchy, and legible technical detail.
- Follow `../../../docs/components.md` for repository implementation work.

### Review existing work

- Lead with the overall verdict and the highest-impact issue.
- Separate strategic, messaging, visual, evidence, and execution feedback.
- Distinguish a true brand conflict from a reasonable channel-specific choice.
- Recommend concrete changes rather than only describing the problem.

### Update the strategy

- Update `../../../docs/brand/budibase-brand-and-gtm.md` when the user makes a
  durable positioning, audience, proof, personality, or design decision.
- Add a dated entry to its decision log.
- Update this skill only when the workflow or review method changes.

## Non-negotiables

- Position Budibase as operational infrastructure for operations and IT teams,
  not as a generic no-code builder or lightweight productivity tool.
- Maintain technical credibility and regulated-enterprise confidence without
  turning Budibase into an interchangeable legacy enterprise brand.
- Retain the open-source, extensible, and deploy-on-your-terms identity.
- Make security, governance, reliability, ownership, and human oversight part
  of the main story when relevant.
- Never publish unsupported customer, compliance, security, or performance
  claims.
