## Give sales credit disputes a grounded review

Bring the CRM record and sales credit policy together when ownership or allocation is disputed. The agent investigates the available context and prepares unresolved conflicts for an accountable decision.

## How it works

A requester submits a sales credit dispute resolution through Slack, Teams, or the Sales Credit Dispute Form, creating a tracked request row. The agent confirms the request type, retrieves context from CRM, commission records, and sales credit policy, checks the relevant policy source, and asks for any missing details in chat.

Where policy permits, the agent completes disputes where assignment history and policy clearly support one outcome and records the outcome without waiting for manual handling. The agent escalates split-credit conflicts, missing activity history, manager override requests, or compensation risk to the responsible owner with a decision summary and supporting context.

An approver reviews the escalation in chat or the Credit Dispute Queue, then approves, rejects, or returns it for more information.

## Where people stay in control

People remain responsible for approval exceptions; risky or ambiguous requests.
