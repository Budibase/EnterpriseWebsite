# Repository Instructions

## Budibase brand and GTM objective

Budibase is becoming the open-source platform enterprise IT uses to build and
run the software behind business operations. Enterprise IT is the primary
customer and accountable platform owner. Functional teams own the use cases,
and teams across the organization use what IT delivers. Regulated,
security-conscious, and operationally complex enterprises are the priority
go-to-market environment.

For every website, design, brand, copy, messaging, GTM, campaign, customer,
sales-enablement, or positioning task:

1. Read `docs/brand/budibase-brand-and-gtm.md` completely before making
   recommendations or changes.
2. Treat that document as the source of truth for audience, positioning,
   personality, design direction, messaging, proof, and reference brands.
3. Optimize for IT delivery leverage, technical credibility, operational
   confidence, security, governance, reliability, deployment flexibility,
   ownership, and human oversight.
4. Preserve Budibase's distinctive forest-green identity and bold,
   operations-focused character.
5. Position Budibase as the platform for building and running the software
   behind enterprise operations, not as a generic no-code builder, citizen-
   development tool, lightweight productivity product, or speculative AI
   product.
6. Use customer names, logos, metrics, quotations, and claims only when their
   public use is confirmed. Never invent supporting evidence.
7. For implementation work, also follow the component conventions in
   `docs/components.md`.
8. Hold freedom and control together: show how IT can deliver more around its
   existing systems while retaining governance, ownership, and operational
   responsibility.
9. Distinguish current product capabilities from roadmap direction, especially
   when discussing Operate, Prove, Projects, Git, agents, or lifecycle control.

When a task changes the durable brand or GTM strategy, update
`docs/brand/budibase-brand-and-gtm.md` and its decision log in the same change.
